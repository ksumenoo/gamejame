Ashdown House, the 14th of November, 1893

Sir,

I am given to understand that you are a photographer of uncommon patience, and that you do not shy from subjects others find disagreeable.

The house is mine no longer in any sense that matters. Something remains in it. The servants will not stay past dusk, and I confess I cannot either.

I ask that you take the house's likeness. All of it. Whatever stands still long enough to be taken.

The darkroom is as my wife left it. You will find all you need there.

Do not linger in the corridor.

— E. Ashdown
